# Land Sterling

**Category:** Consulting. A RICS valuation and advisory house, not a software play.
**Market:** UAE-headquartered, international. Offices in Abu Dhabi, Algiers, Dubai,
Leeds, London, Riyadh. 150+ consultants, 17 years, US$36B+ of projects valued,
15,000+ assignments.
**Studied from:** a real Land Sterling proposal to Samarth Developer for a Highest
& Best Use study of a residential plot in Dubai South (ref 2026/VALSC/0302, dated
13 July 2026). Facts extracted into this file. The 66-page PDF sits with the session
upload, not committed here.

## The one thing to notice first
Land Sterling quotes **AED 73,500** for essentially the same job AIRE quotes at AED
20,000, and delivers it **faster** (10-12 working days against AIRE's three weeks).
The premium is not speed and not technology. It is trust. Land Sterling is a
chartered valuation firm whose reports 30+ GCC banks accept and the Big 4 recognise.
When you buy Land Sterling you are buying a signed, RICS-standard opinion a lender
will lend against. That is the whole product, and it is worth reading against AIRE's
tech-and-data pitch. Two consultants, same study, opposite value propositions.

## What they are
Land Sterling is primarily a property valuation house (RICS Red Book, IVSC, CIOB
accredited) that also runs a Strategic Consultancy line doing feasibility studies,
highest-and-best-use studies, and market research. Their case-study book is heavily
HBU and valuation work across the UAE: vacant plots in Majan, JVC, Al Furjan, Al
Shahama, Abu Dhabi; feasibility for residential schemes in Sharjah and RAK; large
portfolio valuations for banks and holding companies.

Unlike AIRE, they make no proprietary-dataset claim. The proposal states plainly
that they use "publicly available data and input from subject matter experts" and
give a view on competition "on a best effort basis." Their edge is chartered
judgement and bank-grade credibility, not a data moat.

## How they run a feasibility study
Seven stages, human-led advisory, from the proposal:

1. **Planning, regulatory and site assessment.** Confirm the development envelope
   (land use, GFA/FAR, height, setbacks, parking), title, boundaries, a site
   inspection, and the Dubai South / DDA approval pathway.
2. **Market and competitor analysis.** Macro and submarket context, demand analysis
   (buyer profiles, end-user vs investor, absorption and take-up velocity),
   competitor and pipeline supply benchmarking (product, amenities, service charges,
   payment plans), and pricing / positioning (achievable AED/sq ft, rents, yields).
3. **Development potential and highest and best use.** The classic four-test HBU:
   legally permissible, physically developable, financially feasible, maximally
   productive. Confirms the optimal use.
4. **Conceptual massing and unit mix.** Indicative developable GFA, gross-to-net
   efficiency, achievable unit count, and a recommended studio / 1BR / 2BR / 3BR
   split benchmarked to demand. Explicitly strategic and indicative, not design.
5. **Infrastructure and utility overview.** High-level serviceability (DEWA, district
   cooling, water, sewerage), access and connectivity, and indicative connection
   cost allowances.
6. **Financial feasibility and returns.** Full development cashflow: cost build-up,
   GDV, profit-on-cost and profit-on-GDV, IRR, NPV, peak funding, equity requirement,
   plus sensitivity to price, cost and absorption, upside and downside scenarios, and
   break-even. Stated as high-level to EBITDA, not IFRS-compliant, and the model is
   not a deliverable.
7. **Risks, constraints and recommendations.** A risk register with mitigations and
   a clear go / no-go acquisition view.

## What they charge and how long it takes
AED 70,000 plus 5% VAT, so AED 73,500 total. 10-12 working days to a draft report
after a 1-2 day mobilisation. 50/50 payment. Deliverable is one colour hardcopy plus
an electronic report. The financial model is withheld, same as AIRE.

## What is real vs marketing
The load-bearing work is analyst legwork and chartered sign-off, not technology. The
market and competitor stage is manual benchmarking of competing schemes, absorption,
payment plans, and service charges from public data and interviews. The HBU stage is
a textbook appraisal framework applied by hand. The credibility that justifies the
premium is genuine and external: RICS/IVSC/CIOB registration, PI insurance, and bank
acceptance of the reports. That is the moat, and it is a moat Solum cannot buy with
code.

See [`../analysis/consultants-vs-solum.md`](../analysis/consultants-vs-solum.md),

## What Solum should not copy
- The AED-73,500, 10-12-day engagement shape. Solum's job is to make this
  unnecessary for screening.
- The withheld, EBITDA-level, not-a-deliverable model. Solum's live, inspectable
  model is the opposite and better choice for a self-serve tool.
- The pretence of a data moat. Land Sterling is honest that it uses public data.
  Solum's edge over both is direct DDA and DLD integration, which is a real data
  advantage neither consultant has wired in.
